## Differences from the plan

- The recovery commands are registered in both gate states, so `src/bindings.ts` stays one file for every state (009's rule); without the service (gate closed) they do nothing, and the bubble actions answer only the `recovery_bubble` window.
- The drag starts from the webview: a pointer move past 3 px on the handle asks the native side, which begins the AppKit session on the panel's content view from AppKit's current mouse event and hides the panel (alpha 0, mouse ignored) instead of a separate NSView handle; the preview image is a one-line label of the transcript. To prove in the native drag matrix (Step 6).
- The setting is frozen at the dictation start (`recovery::app::dictation_starting`) and bound to the dictation id at commit (`dictation_started`); an unbound or other dictation classifies nothing (insert as usual), which answers the round 8 review warning on where the setting is captured.
- Classification detail: for a known non-text role, an edit attribute the element does not support (or has no value for) counts as not settable; a timeout or another error is a doubt (`Insert`). `AXGroup` stays undetermined. Initial role lists to calibrate with the Step 1 matrix.
- Surface arbiter: presenting the bubble ends any notice hold (005 AI failure, 007 hand-over) and hides the indicator (`overlay::yield_to_recovery`); a new dictation closes the bubble before the indicator shows. The plan left that precedence open (round 8 warning); decision to confirm with Julien (checklist §3).
- The indicator stops raising 006's destination notice; `notice-model.ts` keeps `destinationNotice` and `parseOutcome` with their tests, no longer called in production.
- The presentation hand-off lives in `destination/presentation.rs` (006 side) to keep `delivery.rs` under 400 lines.
- 007's `macos_cf` helpers are `pub(crate)` so the classifier reuses its CoreFoundation ownership instead of a copy.
- The preview is the first 240 characters of the transcript (`PREVIEW_MAX_CHARS`); the view clamps it to two lines with an ellipsis. Copy and drag carry the full transcript from the native manager.
- The Penflow design gate (design certification before UI code) is BLOCKED (`code-ir.json` absent), as for 009: the UI follows the approved mockups and the contract test IDs of the base screen `recovery-bubble` (state variants as `data-state`/`data-reason`, explanation IDs of `recovery-bubble-explanation`); certification stays pending.
